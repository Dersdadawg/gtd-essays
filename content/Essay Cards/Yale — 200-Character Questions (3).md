## Prompt

> [!question]+ Prompt
> Please respond in no more than 200 characters (approximately 35 words or fewer) to each:

> 2. What is one aspect of yourself that you hope to grow or develop during college?
> 3. What is something about you that is not included anywhere else in your application?
